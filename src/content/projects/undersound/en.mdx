## The challenge

Independent artists get little room on the big platforms. Undersound is a social network for underground music where anyone can publish their work and build an audience.

## The solution

A full web application built with **Next.js 16**, **Supabase** and **Stripe**:

- **User accounts** with sign-up, login and artist profiles.
- **Song and album uploads**, with an **audio player** that keeps playing while you browse.
- **Feed and explore** pages with search and genre filters.
- **Social features:** follow artists, like, comment and get notifications.
- **Monetization:** Stripe payments to promote a song (*boost*), a Pro plan and webhooks that confirm every payment.
- **Moderation:** users can report inappropriate content.

## What I learned

How to design a relational database with row-level security, handle real payments safely with webhooks and build a continuous playback experience in a React app.
