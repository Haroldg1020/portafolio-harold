## The client

Harold Ocampo is a lawyer in Cali, Colombia, who provides preventive legal advice under the **NovaDiligencia** brand: he verifies the property, the parties and the contract before his clients sign or hand over money in a real estate deal.

## The challenge

He needed a digital presence that conveyed trust and seriousness, explained complex legal services in plain language and turned visits into consultation requests.

## The solution

- **Visual identity:** horizontal logo and symbol, with variants for light, dark and single-color backgrounds.
- **Next.js website**, deployed on Vercel, with a page per service: asset forfeiture checks, property due diligence, verification of the parties, contracts and support through closing.
- **A 7-step process** that shows clients how the work is done, from the first consultation to follow-up.
- **Conversion:** consultation request form and direct contact via WhatsApp and email.
- **Blog** to rank on Google for topics the target client searches for.
- **Legal compliance:** personal data policy under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012.

## Outcome

A fast, professional site that the lawyer uses as his main channel to get clients.
